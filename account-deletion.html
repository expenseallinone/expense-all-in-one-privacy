Expense All In One - Account Deletion

This page explains how users of Expense All In One can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Account deletion is available.

To request deletion of your Expense All In One account and associated personal data, send an email to:

expenseallinone@gmail.com

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your Expense All In One account.
  2. Use the subject: Expense All In One - Account Deletion Request
  3. Include your username, user ID, or email address associated with your Expense All In One account.
  4. Our support team will review and verify the request.
  5. After verification, the account deletion request will be processed.

Data That Will Be Deleted

After a verified account deletion request is processed, personal account information and associated user data stored for the account will be deleted, subject to applicable legal, security, and regulatory requirements.

  • Account information
  • User profile information
  • Cloud-synced expense information associated with the account
  • Other personal information associated with the account, where applicable

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained when required for legal, regulatory, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes.

Any information that must be retained will only be kept for the period required by the applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Local Data Stored on Your Device

Some data may be stored locally on your device. Account deletion from our online services may not automatically remove data that is stored only on your device.

To remove locally stored application data, use the data-clearing options provided by your Android device or uninstall the Expense All In One application.

Deletion Processing

Account deletion requests are processed after verification of the request. We will take reasonable steps to process verified deletion requests within a reasonable period.

Important: Account deletion may permanently remove account-associated data and may not be reversible after processing.

Contact Us

If you have questions about account deletion or your personal data, contact the Expense All In One support team at:

expenseallinone@gmail.com
